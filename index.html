A few things you may be wondering

Before we
set sail.

A little clarity on what’s coming, and what we’re still working through.

When will Cubernity launch?

The launch date is not yet confirmed. We plan to start with a focused pilot before wider availability. Once registrations open, waitlist members will receive an update before launch.

What will it cost?

Pricing is coming soon. Plans, pilot terms and any founding-member benefits will be shared before you make a commitment.

How will offline work and sync behave?

Supported workflows are being designed to save changes locally and sync when connectivity returns. Carrier lookups, filings and other external services require a connection. Offline scope and conflict-resolution behavior will be confirmed during the pilot; conflicting changes should be reviewed rather than silently overwritten.

How will you protect our data?

Encryption, role-based permissions, audit trails and backups are design priorities. We will share implemented controls, hosting regions, retention policies and applicable processing terms before onboarding. No certification is being claimed on this page.

Which integrations are planned?

Carrier APIs and EDI, customs portals, accounting tools and messaging services are on the roadmap. Specific providers, such as Tally, Zoho, SAP or QuickBooks, will depend on pilot needs and technical availability. No provider partnership or live connection is implied.

Can we migrate from Excel?

We plan guided imports for supported data using structured templates. Data mapping, validation and migration scope will be discussed during onboarding. Keep a backup of your original records.

Which regions and teams will you support?

We’re designing with India-based and global freight workflows in mind. Initial regional availability, regulatory support and languages will be confirmed with pilot partners. The site is currently available in English.

Is the product preview a live platform?

No. This is a coming-soon website. Dashboard figures are fictional, the offline demonstration is a simulation, and the roadmap describes planned capabilities.
