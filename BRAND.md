# Cubernity — brand direction

**Brand name:** Cubernity

**Website:** https://cubernity.com/

**Tagline:** Every shipment. One connected flow.

## Final logo: Connected C

An open, geometric C makes Cubernity’s initial the center of the identity. Its angular outline suggests a container frame, while the teal segment forms a continuous route ending in a connection point. The open right side keeps the mark legible and gives it a sense of forward movement.

The mark uses original geometric SVG paths, with no clip-art vessel or third-party brand asset. Use the icon alone at small sizes and the horizontal lockup for larger placements. The navy-and-teal version is for light surfaces; the reversed version is for dark surfaces. The main favicon uses a white-and-teal mark on navy.

## Brand guidelines

- **Clear space:** keep at least one stroke width (6 units in the 48-unit master) clear on all sides.
- **Minimum sizes:** icon 16px for favicon use; 24px preferred for UI. Horizontal lockup 180px wide. At smaller widths, use the inline wordmark or icon.
- **Palette:** navy #0B1F3A; ocean blue #1E6FFF; teal #00C2A8; white #FFFFFF; light gray #F3F7F8. Use #007967 for small teal text on white. Light mint supports hero accents.
- **Type:** modern sans; Inter preferred with Avenir/system fallbacks. The standalone website does not make Google Fonts requests. SVG wordmarks retain editable text and may vary by installed font; outline approved lettering for final production assets.
- **Colorways:** full color for light backgrounds, navy monochrome for single-ink light applications, white reversed for dark backgrounds. The favicon adds a contrasting tile.
- **Do:** preserve aspect ratio, keep clear space, use the reversed mark on navy and verify small-size legibility.
- **Don’t:** stretch, rotate, recolor arbitrarily, add shadows to the mark, place it on a busy background, or use teal small text where contrast is insufficient.
