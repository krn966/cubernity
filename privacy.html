Your data deserves clarity.

This is a privacy notice placeholder, not a finalized legal policy. The launch waitlist and NVOCC partner register use separate Formspree forms as described below.

Who is responsible?

Cubernity is the website and platform brand. Before collecting data, add the registered legal entity, postal address, privacy contact and any applicable representative or grievance contact.

What this preview does

The waitlist sends the information you enter to Formspree when you submit it with the contact-consent checkbox selected. The offline demonstration contains fictional data held temporarily in page memory. Local storage remembers theme and privacy choices. A submission timestamp supports a basic rate limit. Hosting providers may process technical request logs according to their own policies.

NVOCC partner registration

When you submit the partner-register form, its eleven fields are sent to Formspree for Cubernity: company name, registered or office address, optional website, contact name, designation, email, phone number, operating container count, service countries, key ports or trade lanes, and optional remarks. Cubernity uses these details to maintain its partner register and follow up about the business relationship. The records are not published on this website or saved in the GitHub repository. The owner accesses submissions through their authenticated Formspree account.

The form sends these details only when you select Submit details. An unsuccessful or unconfirmed request leaves the entries in the current page so you can retry. Formspree processes submissions under its privacy policy. Do not include confidential shipment or customer records in remarks.

Launch waitlist information

The waitlist collects: name, work email, company, role, country or region, monthly shipment range, selected tools, an optional pain-point description and your contact consent. Cubernity uses this information to manage the requested launch and pilot communications. It is submitted to Formspree and is not published in the GitHub repository. Do not include confidential shipment or customer information in the form.

Consent and your choices

The form asks for affirmative consent to launch and pilot communications. Publish a working withdrawal method before enabling it. Optional analytics are disabled in this version. You can reopen Privacy choices in the footer; clearing browser site data removes locally saved preferences.

Retention, processors and transfers

Before enabling registration, specify the retention period, deletion process, form and hosting providers, processing locations and any applicable international-transfer protections. Do not retain leads indefinitely. Limit access to authorized people and document safeguards.

Applicable data protection laws

Prepare the final notice and operational processes for applicable requirements under the GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, including notice, consent where applicable, withdrawal and request handling. Confirm applicable provisions and obligations with qualified counsel before launch. This placeholder does not claim compliance.

Requests and complaints

Add a verified contact for access, correction, erasure, withdrawal and other applicable requests, along with the relevant complaint and grievance process. Do not collect personal data until these details and the final policy are available.
