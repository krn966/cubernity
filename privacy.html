Your data deserves clarity.

This is a privacy notice placeholder, not a finalized legal policy. Registrations are disabled until Cubernity configures a collection service and publishes the applicable notice.

Who is responsible?

Cubernity is the website and platform brand. Before collecting data, add the registered legal entity, postal address, privacy contact and any applicable representative or grievance contact.

What this preview does

The preview does not send or save the information entered into its waitlist form. The offline demonstration contains fictional data held temporarily in page memory. Local storage remembers theme and privacy choices. When a signup endpoint is enabled, a submission timestamp supports a basic rate limit. Hosting providers may process technical request logs according to their own policies.

What registration would collect

Once enabled: name, work email, company, role, country or region, monthly shipment range, selected tools, an optional pain-point description and your contact consent. Use this information only to manage the requested launch and pilot communications. Do not include confidential shipment or customer information in the form.

Consent and your choices

The form asks for affirmative consent to launch and pilot communications. Publish a working withdrawal method before enabling it. Optional analytics are disabled in this version. You can reopen Privacy choices in the footer; clearing browser site data removes locally saved preferences.

Retention, processors and transfers

Before enabling registration, specify the retention period, deletion process, form and hosting providers, processing locations and any applicable international-transfer protections. Do not retain leads indefinitely. Limit access to authorized people and document safeguards.

Applicable data protection laws

Prepare the final notice and operational processes for applicable requirements under the GDPR and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, including notice, consent where applicable, withdrawal and request handling. Confirm applicable provisions and obligations with qualified counsel before launch. This placeholder does not claim compliance.

Requests and complaints

Add a verified contact for access, correction, erasure, withdrawal and other applicable requests, along with the relevant complaint and grievance process. Do not collect personal data until these details and the final policy are available.
